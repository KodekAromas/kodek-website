About Kodek

Built for depth.
Built for the frontier.

Kodek (Kodekaromas Technologies Private Limited) is an AI and technology research company headquartered in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. We build intelligent software, immersive simulations, AR/VR experiences, and interactive games that push the boundaries of what software can do.

Our work spans five technology verticals — unified by a single research-oriented identity. We are not a software agency. We are a company that uses artificial intelligence, simulation, and immersive computing to solve real problems across industries.

Every research direction at Kodek leads to a working product or deployable prototype. We build real AI systems, real simulations, real engines — not wrappers or templates. India-built. Globally competitive.
