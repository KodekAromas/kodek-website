About Kodek

Built for depth.
Built for the frontier.

Kodek (Kodekaromas Technologies Private Limited) is an AI and technology research company headquartered in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. We build intelligent software, immersive simulations, AR/VR experiences, and interactive games.

We are not a software agency. We are a company that uses artificial intelligence, simulation, and immersive computing to solve real problems across industries. Every research direction leads to a working, deployable product. India-built. Globally competitive.
